Privacy Policy

The Money Plugins portfolio currently includes ScopeGuard, BidReady, RefundRadar, InvoiceChaser, GrantFit, VendorGuard, DealDesk, RenewalRadar, TravelClaim, and CashLeak.

Version 1.0 processes information sent to its MCP endpoints only to return the requested result and does not intentionally persist prompt or document content in an application database.

Hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical logs such as request timing, network information, and error diagnostics under their own policies. Do not submit passwords, full payment-card numbers, government identifiers, or other secrets.

Some plugins support commercial, financial, contract, or claims workflows. Their outputs are decision support and should be checked against authoritative contracts, policies, regulations, grant documents, tender documents, or professional advice when appropriate.

For privacy questions, use the support page.
