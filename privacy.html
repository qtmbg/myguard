Privacy Policy

ScopeGuard, BidReady and RefundRadar process information sent to their MCP endpoints only to return the requested result. Version 1.0 does not intentionally persist prompt or document content in an application database.

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical logs such as timestamps, request metadata, network information and error diagnostics under their own policies. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, government identifiers or other secrets unless a future authenticated feature explicitly requires and protects them.

RefundRadar provides policy-based decision support and is not legal advice. BidReady provides decision support and does not guarantee an award. ScopeGuard provides pricing calculations based on user-supplied economics.

For privacy questions, use the support page.
