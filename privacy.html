Privacy Policy

The Money Plugins portfolio currently includes ScopeGuard, BidReady, RefundRadar, InvoiceChaser, GrantFit, VendorGuard, DealDesk, RenewalRadar, TravelClaim, and CashLeak.

Version 1.0 processes information sent to its MCP endpoints only to return the requested result and does not intentionally persist prompt text, document content, or tool arguments in an application database.

For reliability, product analytics, and abuse prevention, the service may log limited technical metadata such as plugin/tool name, success or failure, latency, locale, and SHA-256 pseudonyms of subject/session/organization identifiers supplied by the ChatGPT host when available. These logs are not intended to contain the user's prompt or tool arguments.

Hosting and infrastructure providers may also process standard technical logs such as request timing, network information, and error diagnostics under their own policies. Do not submit passwords, full payment-card numbers, government identifiers, or other secrets.

Some plugins support commercial, financial, contract, or claims workflows. Their outputs are decision support and should be checked against authoritative contracts, policies, regulations, grant documents, tender documents, or professional advice when appropriate.

Recipients: QTMBG LLC and Vercel as the hosting provider. Tool inputs are processed in memory for the request and are not stored in an application database. Technical runtime logs are retained for the hosting plan window, which ranges from 1 hour to at most 30 days under Vercel's current retention limits; no separate analytics archive is created by this service. Pseudonymous identifiers remain technical personal data, not guaranteed anonymous data.

You can avoid supplying identifying details and can contact QTMBG LLC through the support page for access, correction, or deletion requests. Because inputs are not stored and host identifiers are hashed, we may be unable to locate a particular request without its timestamp. OpenAI and Vercel independently operate their own platform logging and controls.
