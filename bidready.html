BidReady

Know if it is worth bidding before you spend a week bidding.

BidReady helps teams review RFPs, tenders, grants and procurement opportunities, identify eligibility gates and mandatory requirements, expose critical gaps, estimate response workload, and produce a structured bid/no-bid decision.

Authoritative tender documents always override extracted assumptions, and BidReady never guarantees eligibility or an award.
