# TravelClaim policy verification

Checked official US DOT and European Union sources on 5 October 2026:
- https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds
- https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm

The synthetic five review cases supply disruption facts only. They omit a route, operating carrier, travel date, and disruption cause. Consequently no compensation or refund entitlement is asserted. The backend severity categories are internal factual triage, not statutory thresholds. A real claim must retrieve current official rules for the itinerary and the current operating carrier policy before asserting rights.

The expense calculator totals supplied receipts only and makes no reimbursement determination. Fabricated receipts must be refused.
